📘 CRM User Guide

1. Getting Started

  • Login: Open your CRM URL and log in with your credentials.
  • Navigation: Use the sidebar/top menu to access modules like Dashboard, Clients, Projects, Tasks, Payments, Bugs.
  • Profile: Update your profile and password from the settings menu.

2. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard gives you a quick snapshot of your business:

  • Filters: Choose Custom (1st–Today), Week, Month, Year.
  • Widgets: See totals for Payments, Projects, Tasks, Bugs.
  • Charts: Visualize Sales, Profit, Revenue, Net Income, and Task Progress.

3. Enterprise Clients Table

This table shows combined data:

  • Task Name + Project ID
  • Paid Status (only payments marked as paid with due date in current month)
  • Payments (₹)
  • Total Tasks & Completed Tasks
  • Progress Bar for completion percentage

Footer totals show overall payments, tasks, completed tasks, and average progress.

4. Managing Clients & Projects

  • Add Client: Enter client details (name, phone, website).
  • Create Project: Add project info (amount, payment terms, policy, reference link, status).
  • Track Payments: Payments linked to projects show paid status and due date.

5. Task Management

  • Create Task: Assign tasks to projects with deadlines.
  • Update Status: Mark tasks as in progress or completed.
  • History: Use discussion/chat section to track updates.

6. Bug Tracking

  • Report Bug: Attach screenshots and describe the issue.
  • Status: Track bugs as open or fixed.
  • Resolution: Developers update bug status once solved.

7. Payments

  • Paid Payments: Only payments with status paid and due date in current month are counted.
  • Advance Payments: Shown separately if applicable.
  • Revenue & Profit: Automatically calculated in dashboard.

8. Tips for Users

  • Always set deadlines for tasks so they appear in reports.
  • Use filters to analyze performance for different time frames.
  • Check Enterprise Clients table for combined view of tasks and payments.
  • Keep project IDs consistent so tasks and payments link correctly.
  • Update bug statuses regularly to maintain clean reports.

9. FAQ

Use the dropdown on the dashboard to select Custom, Week, Month, or Year. Custom defaults to 1st of the month to today.

Payments only count if they are marked as paid and the due date is within the current month. Otherwise, the amount will show as 0.

10. Support

If you face issues:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Ensure you are logged in with correct credentials.
  • Contact your CRM administrator for technical help.
